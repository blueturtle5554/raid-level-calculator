How are the results calculated?

All players start with zero unspent Immortal Souls and receive the same daily clan reward. Every player buys upgrades as soon as they can afford them.

One regular raid and one purchased bonus raid are completed per day, for a total reward of 200 × raid level. The top player determines the maximum beatable raid, and the raid increases as that player levels up.

Player damage is 10 × 3(player level − 1) per click, with 880 clicks per raid. Boss health is 17,500 × p × 3ceil(raid level / 3). The values of p for Raids 1–6 are 0.1, 0.3, 0.5, 0.2, 0.4, and 0.6; starting at Raid 7, they repeat 0.3, 0.5, 0.7.

The upgrade from level L costs 150(3L² + L). The calculators assume the highest beatable raid can be selected immediately, rather than unlocking raid levels one by one.

Days are counted as complete daily raid rewards. The catch-up result is the first day both players have the same level; their unspent soul balances can still differ.
